# Research result

**Status: Partial**

The official AT-LIF identity is current-based \(o\in\{0,\theta\}\), and with thresholds frozen that scale is absorbed into the next layer’s weights, so transmitted spikes stay binary. [S1][S2] Prosperity (HPCA 2025), GustavSNN (HPCA 2026), LoAS (MICRO 2024), Phi and Bishop (ISCA 2025), and FireFly-S (TCAS-I) remain complete priors for that binary-spike GeMM path: \(\{0,1\}\) activations select multi-bit weights by accumulate-or-skip, then IF/LIF emits the next binary map. [S7][S8][S9][S10][S11][S12] They do not cover a noncausal \(T{=}10\) mix of pre-threshold activations or a separate continuous residual/PED tensor beside that GeMM. [S13][S19] Same-name adaptive-threshold LIF, membrane-dependent integer/graded fire, and ternary or per-event analog-payload “AT-LIF” variants are not this absorbable binary identity. [S4][S5][S6]

### Absorbable official AT-LIF, not a graded spike

AT-LIF output is \(o_i[t]=\theta_i\cdot H(m_i[t]-\theta_i)=\theta_i s_i[t]\), written as \(o_i[t]\in\{0,\theta_i\}\) rather than a common LIF \(0\)–\(1\) spike. Scaling by \(\theta\) does not change the binary nature of the event; a layer-shared frozen threshold is folded into the subsequent synaptic weights, so the deployed model does not need graded-spike hardware. [S3] The official module stores one scalar threshold and the surrogate returns a Heaviside mask times that scalar, not a membrane-dependent analog grade.

### Identities that must be excluded

KBS 2025 AT-LIF (Ai et al.) is a different neuron: it raises the firing threshold after discharge to suppress over-activation, and is cited for that plasticity against standard Heaviside \(S\in\{0,1\}\), not as \(o=\theta s\) folded into \(W\). I-LIF’s forward fire is \(\mathrm{Clip}(\mathrm{round}(V_{\mathrm{pre}}),0,D)\in\{0,\ldots,D\}\) per event, and Overflow emits a membrane-minus-threshold grade plus a boost; those event-varying amplitudes are not a frozen \(\theta\) times a binary \(s\). Local ternary AT-LIF returns \(\{-\theta,0,+\theta\}\) (official AT-LIF mode forbids non-binary output); an int8 HBG-RP ATLIF contract keeps a non-absorbable per-event payload and disclaims identity with NeurIPS \(\{0,\theta\}\); Guo et al. \(\{-\alpha,0,\alpha\}\) is absorbable signed ternary, still not official binary AT-LIF.

### Complete priors for the binary-spike GeMM path

Prosperity’s Spiking GeMM concatenates binary spike matrices in \(\{0,1\}\) against a shared weight matrix and replaces multiply with sparse addition (accumulate the matching weight if the bit is \(1\), skip if \(0\)), and that operation is more than \(98\%\) of SNN ops. GustavSNN is the same path end-to-end: input \(B\in\{0,1\}^{D\times N\times T}\), synaptic weights accumulate when a binary spike occurs, then threshold-and-reset yields output \(C\in\{0,1\}^{M\times N\times T}\). LoAS replaces ANN MAC with bitwise-AND plus accumulate on unary \(0/1\) spikes, then LIF-thresholds the partial sum to emit \(1\) or \(0\) (accumulate the corresponding nonzero, or discard it). Phi treats binary \(1\)-spike / \(0\)-non-spike matmul as weight accumulation only (AC, not MAC) and, on a matching row, reuses an offline pattern\(\times\)weight GeMM. Bishop’s MLP and projection PEs execute Select-ACcumulation (MUX plus accumulator) of multi-bit weights selected by binary spikes; Q/K/V use binary \(X\), the last SSA LIF is moved before the output linear so \(O_{\mathrm{attn}}\) is multiplication-free on binary LIF activations, and the attention core is AND-accumulate/SAC on binary Q/K/V. FireFly-S absorbs shared per-channel quantization scales into membrane thresholds so weight accumulation, bias add, and threshold compare need no runtime rescaling, runs integer-only arithmetic, and skips zeros on both weights and binary spikes.

### Not a prior: noncausal \(T{=}10\) mix before threshold

These datapaths accumulate or reuse after binary \(\{0,1\}\) spikes already exist; they do not apply \(H=WX\) with \(W\in\mathbb{R}^{T\times T}\) to pre-threshold activations, and they do not use \(T{=}10\) as that mix. Prosperity unrolls already-binary per-timestep spike maps into extra GeMM rows that share weights (transformer spike-matrix shape \((T\times L,\,d_i)\)), then a LIF array produces the next binary map. [S14] GustavSNN’s mix is causal LIF of binary presynaptic spikes, \(v_j(t)=\lambda v_j(t-1)+\sum_i w_{ij}S_i(t)\), fire-and-subtract when \(v_j\ge V_\theta\); CPTB/NRV operate on binary spike submatrices at a tick, then \(C_k=\mathrm{LIF}(O_k)\); evaluated lengths include \(4\), \(24\), and \(40\), not a \(T{=}10\) PSN identity. [S15] LoAS spatially unrolls the timestep loop of unary spike\(\times\)weight accumulations, then fires with previous-step membrane \(U[t-1]\) (\(X=O+U[t_{i-1}]\)); default experimental \(T\) is \(4\), and the contrast is Stellar FS neurons, not PSN. [S16] FireFly-S, Phi, Bishop, SpikeX, ExSpike, and Spike-IAND-Former likewise mix after binary spikes (weight accumulate, pattern-weight products of \(0\)–\(1\) vectors, AND-accumulate, or full-event conv so layer inputs stay spike-based) and then IF/LIF; Spike-IAND-Former’s \(T\)-parallel unit is unrolled causal LIF muxed \(111/101/000\) for \(T{=}4/2/1\) (CIFAR-10 \(95.69/92.93/91.34\)), not a dense \(T\times T\) pre-threshold mix. [S17]

The noncausal \(T\times T\) mix sits in the PSN neuron (NeurIPS 2023): \(H=WX\) on the input sequence, unmasked \(W\) lets \(H[t]\) see future \(X[i]\), then binary \(S=\Theta(H-B)\). Masked PSN zeros future weights for step-by-step inference. Image-classification PSN uses \(T{=}4\); sliding PSN on CIFAR10-DVS reports \(T{=}4,8,10\) (\(85.90\%\) at \(T{=}10\)). SDformerFlow-v2 switches to that PSN (\(H=WX\), \(S=\Theta(H-B)\)) at \(10\) time steps. The hardware-venue GeMM priors above do not implement it. [S18]

### Not a prior: a separate continuous residual/PED tensor

Prosperity’s main path is binary-spike Spiking GeMM into a LIF array that emits the next layer’s spikes; it does not describe a second continuous residual or PED tensor consuming that GeMM source. ExSpike’s shortcut is spike-only: Residual Spike SRAM stores source spike feature maps, then each EPE cluster accumulates weights, updates membrane, and fires binary spikes. [S20] In LoAS, “residual” is leftover membrane after a hard-reset fire, \(U[t_i]=\tau X[t_i](1-C[t_i])\), carried to the next timestep—not a parallel continuous feature/PED tensor beside the unary GeMM. [S21] SpikeX’s residual is leaky membrane voltage \(\lambda u[t-1]\); each layer’s input and output spike \(S\) is binary, and synaptic current is a weighted sum of those binary events. [S22] FireFly-T allows post-neuron spike addition or pre-neuron membrane-potential addition and adopts the latter so convolutions still see spike-based inputs; the overlay is orchestrator plus sparse (spike\(\times\)weight) engine plus binary attention, with residual I/O as a feature-map port, not a continuous PED GeMM. [S23] Bishop moves LIF before the last SSA linear specifically to avoid multiplier-heavy spike-residual arithmetic, so that product is binary LIF activations times \(W_o\). [S24]

## Coverage and uncertainty
- "Question 1 uncertainty: The KBS 2025 AT-LIF PDF body (equations for the spike value itself) was not readable beyond the abstract/citation pages, so it is excluded from the NeurIPS {0, θ} identity on the described threshold-after-discharge mechanism, not on a verified analog-output equation."
- "Question 1 uncertainty: Per-neuron (non-shared) frozen θ_j is still column-scalable as W'[:,j] = W[:,j] θ_j; the NeurIPS authors only claim absorption for a single shared layer threshold, so whether they would treat per-neuron θ as the same official identity is unstated."
- "Question 1 uncertainty: No inspected primary AT-LIF paper claims a membrane-remainder or int8 per-event analog as the official AT-LIF inference interface; that analog contract appears only as a local co-design disclaimer against the NeurIPS identity."
- "Question 2 uncertainty: No inspected ISSCC 2024–2026 paper claims an exclusive complete digital binary-spike GeMM path: ISSCC 2024 20.5 C-Transformer is a homogeneous DNN-Transformer/Spiking-Transformer hybrid (HMAU multiply in DT-mode, N accumulations in ST-mode); ISSCC 2024 30.2 is sparsity-adaptive analog/IMC, not digital GeMM accumulate."
- "Question 2 uncertainty: ESTU (IEEE TCAS-II, Dec 2025, 10.1109/TCSII.2025.3626209) states binary spikes let MAC be replaced by additions, but the datapath still includes “Two multipliers used for Dense(int)”, so it is not a complete multiply-free binary-spike GeMM path."
- "Question 2 uncertainty: CICC 2024 Fang et al. (10.1109/CICC60959.2024.10529019) IEEE abstract claims SNN “accumulation (AC) features” and a “spiking-only accelerator,” but the full paper PDF was not inspected, so a complete {0,1} GeMM formulation and any threshold/weight folding were not verified."
- "Question 2 uncertainty: No MICRO 2025 dedicated SNN/binary-spike GeMM accelerator was located; MICRO 2024 LoAS is the in-window MICRO primary above."
- "Question 2 uncertainty: CICC 2025 sample-wise-adaptive SNN (10.1109/CICC65396.2025.10983259) was inspected only at abstract level (dynamic neuron pruning / SOP reduction), not as a stated complete binary-spike GeMM+folding path."

- "Question 3 uncertainty: In PSN, X is the neuron input-current sequence (typically already a weighted sum of the previous layer’s spikes); the T×T mix is of those currents into H before this neuron’s threshold, not a mix of analog activations in place of binary S. Combining AT-LIF {0,θ} with T=10 PSN as one primary-paper identity is not stated in PSN or SDformerFlow."
- "Question 4 uncertainty: The token “PED” does not appear in the inspected Prosperity, ExSpike, SpikeX, LoAS, Phi (2505.10909), Bishop, FireFly-T, or FireFly-S (2408.15578) texts; absence of the name is not by itself a proof they never store a non-spike tensor, but no PED/continuous-residual-GeMM path is specified in the sections read."
