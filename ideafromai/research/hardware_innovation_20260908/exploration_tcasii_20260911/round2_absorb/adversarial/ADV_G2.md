## 4. Attack

### 4.1 ExSpike already does adjacent AND

G2 writes A as “ExSpike adjacent AND” and then proposes 2-D edge-runs (I2) and motion-translated neighbor groups (I1) as X. That is the same reused object.

ExSpike APEC (R2L1 §4; local `2606.20414.txt` §III-A2): group adjacent spatial positions, reuse the **intersection** of their channel-sets

\[
O_G=\bigcap_{i=1}^{g} S_i,\qquad \Delta C=(g-1)|O_G|C_o k^2.
\]

Default **g=2** is best in their Fig. 7 (throughput +10.9% / +13.8% / +14.5% / +11.2% on four CIFAR nets; event reduction 1.35–1.62×). Prosperity **explicitly refused** this intersection case for GeMM rows (§III-B: would need a new row \(A\)). After absorb, APEC is legal **A** on `sn2` / `proj.sn` binary maps. R2L1’s order is: *copy both As separately; do not retitle APEC as “spatial ProSparsity.”*

| G2 parent | Why it is APEC-class, not X |
|---|---|
| Q05-I2 8-connected edge-run | Adjacent 1s on a 2-D support. APEC’s observation is exactly “adjacent spatial positions often exhibit overlapping spike sequences.” Streaming select-add along runs is full-event EConv along a chain — ExSpike’s dataflow, not a new ALU. |
| Q05-I1 translated NRV | If DSEC displacement is O(1) pixel, translation **is** adjacency. APEC g=2 already ANDs that pair. If displacement is large, adjacent AND misses the match — but then you need a **warp** (attack 4.3–4.4), not a bigger APEC group (their own §III-A2: \(|O_G|\) falls as \(g\) grows; gain is not monotonic). |
| Q01-I6 XOR-delta | Temporal neighbor of the same index. ExSpike/FireFly already skip zeros each \(t\). XOR of *shifted* edges is denser than either bitmap (P04-I6 objection; source-lag probe: lag-1/3 difference **raised** nonzeros 69.67%/41.27%). |

ExSpike Fig. 8 is the same family of failure this freeze already measured: APEC-2 **cuts calculation cycles and raises Weight-ready cycles**, wiping the win on some blocks. Arithmetic ↓ does not imply occupancy ↓. Lifting already did this on the student: always-ready −22.83%, long-BP **both 8088**. A 2-D run-length decoder that “lengthens bursts” is predicted to die the same way unless it moves the **residual/BN** port, which G2 explicitly does not schedule.

Phi (R2L3) is a further A on post-absorb binary rows: L1/L2 *pattern* hierarchy of spike supports. Edge-run is a 2-D pattern. FireFly-S CLK0 is already a 3×3 spatial Bitmap AND (their study shape is a conv). G2-I2’s “flat AND still walks empty 2-D holes” is FireFly’s idle-cycle problem; they fill it with a bias bubble, they do not invent edge-chains as a title.

**Attack result:** spatial co-fire of binary spikes after absorb is **A**. Naming ExSpike in the A line and then selling adjacency as X is a reskin the fusion card itself predicts.

### 4.2 Prosperity is combinatorial, not geometric — and that is the point of A

Prosperity’s reuse theorem is a **set** theorem (R2L1 §1.1):

\[
S_i=\{j\mid M[i,j]=1\},\quad
\text{EM: }S_i=S_j,\quad
\text{PM: }S_j\subsetneq S_i,\quad
\text{intersection refused}.
\]

Product of a row depends only on the **set of firing columns**, not on where those rows sat in the image, not on optical flow, not on epipolar geometry. Two (t,h,w) sites with the same support share the product against the same absorbed \(W'\) whether they are neighbors, motion-translated copies, or opposite corners of a tile. Time is extra independent rows sharing \(W\), not a 2-D warp.

What G2 actually changes, if it never drops a live spike, is the **order and tile membership** of those rows so that sliding edges become EM/PM pairs or NRV co-live columns. That is:

- Prosperity Dispatcher: they already **reorder** by popcount (stable sort, prefix-before-suffix). A motion-aware sort key is a different temporal law for the **same forest**. Checklist step 5, not X. R2L1 §6: *“1RW vs TCAM is not automatically X. Building a 1RW Detector for the same forest is how you finish A. X begins only if the thing you schedule is no longer prefix \(Y\) of binary rows.”* Motion-aware tiling is the same sentence with “Detector” replaced by “tile.”
- Gustav CPTB: columns are an arbitrary partition \(B=[B_0|\cdots|B_{K-1}]\). Motion-aligned \(P\)-windows are a **partition choice**. NRV payload is still “row index + \(P\)-bit occupancy **this tick**.” Translating the window does not change the skip unit. R2L2 §3.1: CPTB/NRV copy 1-1 onto the binary GeMM; they do not become geometric by picking a different \(k\).
- FireFly AND: already spatial. Restricting AND to a search ROI (I7) is masked GeMM of the algorithm’s existing neighborhood, or an AEE-changing crop.

B-line “Prosperity/FireFly never evaluated 2D flow” is **true and not X**. R2W1 Query 5 is a clean null: copying those priors onto DSEC OF is a new **mapping of A**, not a reskin of an existing OF paper, and not a license to retitle the mapping as the letter. R2L1 one-sentence verdict: *“A letter whose mechanism is product sparsity on the absorbed spike path has no increment, even if the copy is complete and lossless.”* G2’s steelman is that letter with a better sort.

Intersection of motion-translated supports (the case that would actually be “geometric product sparsity”) is the case Prosperity **refused** and ExSpike **took**. You do not get a third theorem by writing \((u,v)\) on the intersection.

Q02-I4, written independently in this same round, already located the only non-reskin geometry object: *“Flow structure lives in the residual/warp, not in the absorbed 0/1 GeMM the forest already knows how to skip.”* If G2 “uses flow to prune spikes by rewriting the forest key, it becomes a reskin of product sparsity and is out.” That kill is internal to the round-2 idea set. G2 as clustered is the reskin Q02-I4 forbade. Geometry that might still be X sits on **consumer B** (residual/PED last-use) — that is **G1**, not G2.

### 4.3 Previous-frame flow is still an oracle (lagged), and C12/H67 are circular or RAFT

Q05’s oracle rule: current-frame *final* flow is illegal; previous frame, C12, coarser H67, ep34/calibration are legal. The attack is that three of those four are either a delayed copy of the answer or an in-graph producer of the GeMM being packed.

**Previous-frame student flow.** r1 is **upstream** of the flow head (`PROBLEM.md` / L6). Frame-\(n\) flow exists only after frame-\(n\) r1+head completed. Using \(F_{n-1}\) to schedule frame-\(n\) r1 is:

- HEVC/H.264 motion-compensated prediction (P04-I3’s own A).
- AMS / P-frame OF compensation of activation maps, which L6 §6 quotes from 2107.07305 §1.1 and **bans** if the flow is current-frame; previous-frame is the same scheduler with a one-window delay.
- SciFlow SCI: warp by the current iteration’s flow — banned as r1 gate (L6 stop table). G2 does the same warp with a stale field.
- E-RAFT warm-start / BAT Table 5 backward-only future flow: **GPU accuracy** priors, allowed by L6 only as M5 *paid next-frame hint that still must retrain r1* and beat copy-previous-flow, static event-count, and zero prior. Not a TCAS-II packing title.

It is still using the **task output** to skip or order work that produces the next output. Calling it “not this-frame final flow” is a timestamp footnote, not a legality proof. Extra RAW: current r1 cannot legally start until \(F_{n-1}\) is written. The motion probe already listed this: *“previous final prediction must be complete before the next early convolution, restricting cross-frame pipeline overlap.”* That is the opposite of moving 8088.

If the steelman keeps the live bitmap as the numerical gate (mis-pack = reorder only), previous flow **cannot skip a MAC**. Then it cannot deliver 15% work reduction; it can only change issue order. If the implementation *does* skip out-of-window spikes (I7 ROI; I3 narrow band), it is an approximate scheduler whose AEE kill is the same as using a bad flow field — and the field that would make AEE safe is the illegal current-final warp (Q05-I1/I7 kill: *only the illegal current-final-flow warp clears both gates*).

**C12 as packer input.** Q05-I1 packs “projection/C12 layers” using C12. Using C12 to pack C12 is circular. Using C12 to pack a later layer adds a RAW on C12 completion; if C12 is itself a binary GeMM after absorb, packing it is A, and the later layer is not the 8088 source.

**H67 coarse-to-fine ROI (I7).** Optical-flow matching *already* has a search neighborhood. Restricting FireFly AND to a coarser-scale window is RAFT/pyramid / ERAFT (ISCAS 2025 FPGA OF) / TCAS-I 2025 direction-prediction. Local OP-STW was already flagged as a non-paper rename of that predictor (`01_ann_sparsity_mechanisms.md`; literature inventory R301). If the student already searched locally, X is implementing the algorithm. If it currently searches full-domain, shrinking the ROI **changes the math** and must pass +0.005; Q05-I7’s own objection is that independent movers grow the legal ROI back to full domain.

**Epipolar I3** is the only parent that is not obviously APEC, Prosperity tiling, or a flow field. Calibration is static. That is stereo geometry, not product sparsity. It still dies as a *letter* on this freeze: (i) after T10 mix, spikes are feature-map channels, not events — Q05-I3’s own objection; (ii) wide calibration band skips too little for 15%; narrow band needs previous disparity ≈ previous flow (attack 4.3 again); (iii) residual/PED/BN are **not** epipolar-gated, so tile skip on a stereo GeMM may not move 8088; (iv) ASNA-Flow (TVLSI 2025) is already a dedicated event-OF SNN ASIC whose IEEE abstract names **temporal sparsity + optical-flow spatial locality** (R2W1 Q3.4). “SNN OF hardware that uses spatial locality” is taken. I3 can remain a **control mask**, not G2’s title.
