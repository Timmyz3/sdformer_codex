             Details of Iterative Update
After performing the spatially adaptive temporal motion
aggregation, we obtain forward fused motion features and
backward fused motion features. These motion features are
then concatenated to form the overall bidirectional motion
features, represented as:

             M bid = Concat(M f wd , M bwd ),               (10)

where M f wd and M bwd denote the forward and backward
fused motion features, respectively, M bid denotes bidirec-
tional overall motion features. Concat(·, ·) represents the
concatenation operation.
   In addition to the bidirectional temporal motion features
M bid , we also use a context network to extract context fea-
tures CF . The context network takes event frames from
timestamp ti − ∆t/N to ti+1 as input, and its architecture is
identical to that of the feature extraction network. We then
concatenate the M bid and CF to form xk . Finally, the iter-
ative update is performed using two ConvGRUs: one with a
