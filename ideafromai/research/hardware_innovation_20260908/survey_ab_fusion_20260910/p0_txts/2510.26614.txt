                                           Abstract— Event cameras are becoming increasingly appeal-
                                        ing for robotic applications. Even though the output of an
                                        event camera is an asynchronous stream of spatially sparse
                                        events, prior works have represented events as synchronous
                                        dense frames or as synchronous voxels. Given a stream of
                                        asynchronous and spatially sparse events, our goal is to discover
                                        an event representation that preserves these properties. We

                                        gesture recognition and object detection. Tokens from Spiking
                                        Patches yield inference times that are up to 3.4× faster than
                                        voxel-based tokens and up to 10.4× faster than frames. We
