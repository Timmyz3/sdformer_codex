   We propose NESTformer, an elastic spiking transformer
that enables runtime granularity control via three elastic
building blocks - MLP, self-attention, and feature extractor.

Architecture, similar to MatFormer [7], where all smaller
configurations are strict subsets of larger ones, enabling
weight sharing and efficient training; (2) Spike-Aware Elas-
ticity where size reduction is designed to minimize both
parameters and, more importantly, spike activity; and (3)
