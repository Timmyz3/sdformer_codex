Beginner notes:

- `AEE` means average endpoint error. Lower is better. It measures optical-flow vector error in pixels.
- `AAE` means average angular error. Lower is better. It measures direction error.
- `PE1`, `PE2`, `PE3` are fractions of pixels with endpoint error above 1, 2, and 3 pixels.
- `outliers` is the outlier fraction used by this evaluation code. Lower is better.
